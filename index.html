Bakery Billing Report Demonstrator

Scenario 1: For a Regular User (B2C) - "Billing Report" as an Invoice or Order History

This is the most common scenario. The "client" is a standard customer, and their "billing report" is their invoice for a specific purchase or a history of all their purchases. This is typically automated by your website's e-commerce or Point of Sale (POS) system.

Automated Steps (Handled by the Website):

  1. User Places Order: The user selects items, goes to checkout, and provides shipping/pickup details and payment information.
  2. System Generates Invoice: The moment the order is confirmed, the system automatically generates a unique invoice. This document includes all the details.
  3. Report Delivery: The user immediately receives an email and can see the order in their account.
